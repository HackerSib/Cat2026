Freelancing on an Online Platform

Service: independent, project-based work (for example web development, graphic design, writing or consulting) found through a digital platform such as Upwork.

About This Career

Freelancers are independent workers who complete short-term “gigs” for clients instead of working for one employer. Examples from our research include freelance consulting, web development and graphic design [1]. Platforms such as Upwork connect freelancers directly to customers, so you can work from home and choose your projects [2].

Remember the trade-offs from the research page: there is no paid leave or medical aid, income can change from month to month, and you must pay your own work expenses.

ICT Devices and Digital Skills Needed

ICT devices

  • A laptop or desktop computer
  • A smartphone for notifications and messages
  • A reliable internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data)
  • Headphones and a microphone for video calls
  • A webcam (often built into a laptop)

Digital skills

  • Using email and a web browser safely
  • Word processing and spreadsheets to write proposals and track income
  • Online communication (chat and video calls)
  • File sharing and cloud storage
  • A specialist skill that clients pay for, such as design or web development
  • Time management and keeping records for tax

How to Register on Upwork

  1. Go to www.upwork.com and choose Sign up, then select “I want to work as a freelancer”.
  2. Enter your name, email address and a strong password, or sign up with a Google account. Confirm your email address.
  3. Choose your main skill or service and list the other skills you offer.
  4. Build your profile: add a professional photo, a title, a short description about yourself, your work experience and your hourly rate.
  5. Add your country and contact details and verify your identity if asked.
  6. Submit your profile for review. When it is approved, search for projects and send proposals to clients.
Note: Platforms change their sign-up screens and rules (for example, most require users to be 18 or older). Always check the official Upwork Help Centre guide to registering as a freelancer before you sign up.
